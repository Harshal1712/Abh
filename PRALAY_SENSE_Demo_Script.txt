BONUS: QUICK RESPONSES FOR COMMON JUDGE INTERRUPTIONS
================================================================================

Q: "What is your model accuracy?"
A: "We focus on F1-score and Recall rather than accuracy because disaster
   events are rare — accuracy would be misleading. We use temporal holdout
   validation: trained on pre-2023 events, tested on 2023-2024 Assam events."

Q: "Is this data real or hardcoded?"
A: "Everything is live. The terrain values come from a 1.92 GB Assam raster
   file read in real-time. The rainfall was fetched from Open-Meteo just
   now. The model is a trained .cbm CatBoost file."

Q: "What if the expert approves a wrong alert?"
A: "Three safeguards: confidence thresholds, the expert gate itself, and the
   Mobile Preview before publishing. Plus citizen ground-truth reports can
   trigger re-assessment if they contradict the alert."

Q: "Why not just use Google Flood Hub?"
A: "Flood Hub is river gauge-based — not terrain + SAR multi-hazard. It
   doesn't provide local language alerts, expert review, or explainable AI.
   We complement it — we fill the last-mile gap."

Q: "Can this work in areas with no internet?"
A: "The terrain raster is pre-computed and stored locally — no internet
   needed for each query. SMS via Twilio works on basic cellular networks,
   no smartphone required for citizens to receive alerts."

Q: "How do you handle cloud cover during monsoon?"
A: "We prioritise Sentinel-1 SAR — it is an active microwave sensor that
   penetrates clouds. SAR is our primary sensor precisely because monsoon
   optical imagery is unreliable."
